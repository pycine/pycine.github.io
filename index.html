01/ About

Close to the metal.
Wide in scope.

I'm Daoudi Mohamed Yacine, a final-year Computer Engineering student at ESPRIT specializing in embedded systems. My work sits at the boundary between software and silicon.

At STMicroelectronics I built a configuration-driven provisioning engine for the Secure Firmware Install toolchain. At Gewinner I developed reusable Zephyr firmware with BLE provisioning, TLS/REST connectivity, and an offline-first filesystem that survives outages and reboots.

I care about deterministic behavior, secure boot, and systems that recover gracefully when the physical world misbehaves — from a first-stage bootloader to an edge model making a decision on a microcontroller.
